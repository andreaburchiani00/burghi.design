Project Name
Częstochowa

Field Editorial
Year 2025
In the summer of 2024, I along with dear friends participated in the Czestochowa pilgrimage; a 2-week Christian Catholic gesture in which starting from Krakow one arrives at the shrine of Jasna Gora. During the whole pilgrimage we shared tent, food, prayer and long walking distances together.

Since the organization suggested not using phones, we brought disposable cameras with us, from which we took a total of 200 photos.
This booklet summarizes the moments of this very dear experience.

Thinking back today to those intense days of prayer, walk has the flavor of a dreamlike experience. Those days so simple and essential, yet charged with a precious memory. The paper so thin wants to remember and evoke part of that delicacy.
