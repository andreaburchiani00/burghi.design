October 2023 was the month of preparation and direction of the POLICATTO video. A video shot in three days around Milan; made with the help of so many and conceived together with dear friends. That video made us proud, united and charged us for a match we had all been waiting too long for.

This match never came again, but this video remains a trace and a memory 
of a work of friendship.
Thank you all, this is a small memory for you.
