Project Name
TBD Ultramagazine
Field UX/UI
Year 2023
TBD – To Be Defined is an ultra-editorial project which provides the publication of a thematic magazine and the collaboration with artists who are invited to react to contents, expressed in the magazine through the presentation of an artwork.

Redesigning the website aimed at promoting and selling TBD's publishing projects; starting with its graphic design and then continuing with interaction and web development.
