Project Name
 TripUp
Field UX/UI
Year 2025
TripUp is the result of a 10-day design challenge. The goal was to think and design an app dedicated to group travel among friends, with management of expenses, commitments and proposals related to the trip.
Skills
 Concept
UX & Architecture
UI design
UI Prototyping
Flow & Interactions
The app is organized in 2 main pages: the Travel page (which serves as the home page) and the Expense page. Both pages are organized and clustered according to trips made and in progress.
In the Navbar, in addition to the two pages of travel and expenses, there is a “+” button, which provides users with the following functions: Receipt, Event, and Survey.

The UI and flows of the app, assuming AI integration, were conceived and designed to reduce interactions to the necessary; optimizing the vacation experience.
