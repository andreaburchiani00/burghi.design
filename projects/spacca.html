Year 2024
DIY / Roma, 2024

Ponte Della Musica has had a strong impact for roman skateboarding, allowing the skate scene to grow to consolidate and to confront. Giving us the possibility to build what we needed the most: a place where we could make this a reality.
I believe it's worth to tell this story; I am proud to be a part of it and I tried to do it as best as I could. Hear me out, this project does not concern only me, it belongs to everyone who helped and contributed its realization, dedicated to you all.

- R.R
