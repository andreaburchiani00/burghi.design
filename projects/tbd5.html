Field Editorial
Year 2026
LAGNE is dedicated to the practices, images and theories of lament. Far from being a mere expression of weakness or passivity, lament permeates rituals, narratives, artistic practices and contemporary technologies, taking the form, in turn, of protest, healing, resistance, irony or assertion.

The fifth issue of TBD Ultramagazine explores lament as a cultural form, a political gesture and an aesthetic device. Through essays, images and interdisciplinary reflections, the issue explores the complexity of lament as a practice capable of redefining the relationships between subjectivity and collectivity, vulnerability and power, voice and listening.
